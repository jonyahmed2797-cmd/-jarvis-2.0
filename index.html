02

SUPER

03

EXTREME

Characters capable of defeating extremely powerful opponents through extraordinary abilities.

04

GOD TIER

Divine, planetary, dimensional or similarly extraordinary levels of power.

05

COSMIC

Characters whose abilities can operate on cosmic, universal or reality-altering scales.

06

Characters whose complete capabilities remain uncertain or constantly evolve.
